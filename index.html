Minecraft JS

A 2D sandbox: dig, collect and rebuild the world.

How to play

  1. Pick a tool from the toolbar. The selected tool is highlighted.
  2. Click a tile in the world.
  3. If the tool matches the tile, the tile disappears from the world.
  4. The removed tile is added to your inventory.
  5. Click an item in the inventory, then click an empty sky tile to place it back.
  6. Press Reset World to restore the original world.

Tools and tiles

ToolRemoves
Axe (A)Tree trunks and leaves
Pickaxe (P)Rock
Shovel (S)Dirt and grass

Using the wrong tool on a tile does nothing, and the tile will shake.
